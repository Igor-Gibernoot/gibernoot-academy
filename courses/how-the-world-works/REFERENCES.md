# References by level (for checking formulas and typical values)

No book is required. Use these only to cross-check a formula or constant a lesson uses and
does not derive; note what was checked in `STATUS.md`.

| Level | Topic | References |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scales, estimation | Weinstein & Adam, *Guesstimation*; Purcell & Morin, *Physics by Estimation* |
| 2 | Forces and motion | Halliday, Resnick & Walker, *Fundamentals of Physics* (mechanics); Feynman Lectures vol. I |
| 3 | Heat | Halliday (thermodynamics); Çengel & Boles, *Thermodynamics*; Callen |
| 4 | Fluids | White, *Fluid Mechanics*; Anderson, *Understanding Flight* |
| 5 | Electricity | Purcell & Morin, *Electricity and Magnetism*; Feynman vol. II |
| 6 | Waves, optics | French, *Vibrations and Waves*; Hecht, *Optics*; Rossing, *Science of Sound* |
| 7 | Matter | Israelachvili, *Intermolecular and Surface Forces*; Atkins, *Physical Chemistry*; Callister, *Materials Science* |
| 8 | Radio | Griffiths, *Introduction to Electrodynamics*; Haykin, *Communication Systems* |
| 9 | Information | Shannon (1948); Cover & Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*; Oppenheim, *Signals and Systems* |
| 10 | Semiconductors | Neamen, *Semiconductor Physics and Devices*; Patterson & Hennessy, *Computer Organization* |
| 11 | Networks, crypto | Kurose & Ross, *Computer Networking*; Tanenbaum; Katz & Lindell, *Introduction to Modern Cryptography* |
| 12 | Software systems | Page et al. (PageRank, 1998); Goodfellow et al., *Deep Learning*; Vaswani et al. (2017) |
| 13 | Space | Wertz & Larson, *Space Mission Analysis and Design*; Sutton, *Rocket Propulsion Elements* |
| 14 | Frontier | Kittel, *Introduction to Solid State Physics*; Griffiths, *Quantum Mechanics* |
