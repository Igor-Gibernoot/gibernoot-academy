# science-faith-mind conventions by chapter

Everything fixed while building this course: notation, running examples, key numbers.
Read only the section you need: `grep -n '^## ' courses/science-faith-mind/CONVENTIONS.md`, then `sed -n` that range.

## General (rules fixed while building)

- Premises numbered P1, P2, ...; conclusion C. Each guided proof asks which premise a critic rejects.
- Label claims: established fact / live debate / faith commitment.
- Bayes: $P(H\mid E)=\dfrac{P(E\mid H)\,P(H)}{P(E)}$; odds form $\dfrac{P(H_1\mid E)}{P(H_2\mid E)}=\dfrac{P(E\mid H_1)}{P(E\mid H_2)}\cdot\dfrac{P(H_1)}{P(H_2)}$.

## Chapter 3

- Emergence lesson: reduction = higher-level fact deducible from parts; weak emergence (Bedau; Life glider, Conway 1937-2020) = deducible in principle, found by running; strong (Broad 1887-1971) = not deducible + downward causation, rejects closure P1; temperature $\tfrac12 m\langle v^2\rangle=\tfrac32kT$, 5 He atoms give 278 K; emergentist brute law vs panpsychist "there from start".

- Panpsychism lesson: panpsychism = basic, simplest-form experience (not thought); structure/dynamics vs intrinsic nature (chess rules vs pieces); proof P1 physics gives only structure, P2 hard problem, P3 intrinsic nature experiential, C; neutral monism (Spinoza 1632-1677 attributes, Russell 1872-1970, 1927); panprotopsychism; combination problem (James 1890); replies constitutive, integrated information (Tononi b.1960), cosmopsychism. Goff b.1981, Strawson b.1952.

- Idealism lesson: kinds subjective (Berkeley 1685-1753), transcendental (Kant 1724-1804), absolute (Hegel 1770-1831), analytic (Kastrup; birth year omitted, 1966 was wrong); Berkeley proof P1 only ideas perceived, P2 ideas in minds, P3 sensible things = what we perceive, C; unthought tree: God vs phenomenalist (Mill); idealist supervenience = physical supervenes on mental, with a Hempel-style dilemma for "mental".

- Dualism lesson: substance (Descartes 1596-1650) vs property (Chalmers b.1966); doubt proof P1 doubt body, P2 cannot doubt thinking, P3 Leibniz's law, C; Kim's closure P1-P3 reused; replies: interactionism drops closure, epiphenomenalism drops P2, parallelism (Leibniz)/occasionalism (Malebranche) deny direct causation; dualists deny supervenience; Elisabeth 1643.

- Materialism lesson: "physicalism" preferred name; supervenience = no two possible worlds alike physically but different mentally; Kim closure proof P1 closure, P2 mental causes, P3 no overdetermination; Hempel 1905-1997 dilemma (current physics false, ideal physics empty), replies structure-and-dynamics and via negativa; Wöhler 1828, Kim 1934-2019. Glossed: physicalism, supervenience, vitalism, causal closure, overdetermination, epiphenomenalism, eliminativist.

- Quantum lesson: spin state $a|{\uparrow}\rangle+b|{\downarrow}\rangle$, Born rule $P=|a|^2$ (running example $0.6, 0.8 \to 0.36, 0.64$); measurement problem = smooth evolution vs one outcome; four views and prices: Copenhagen (device/system line), many worlds (Everett 1957; probability), pilot wave (de Broglie 1927, Bohm 1952; nonlocality), QBism (state = beliefs); all four agree on predictions.

- Bell lesson (guided problem, 24 cards): singlet $E(x,y)=-\cos(x-y)$, $E=P_{\text{same}}-P_{\text{opposite}}$; local sheet $A,A',B,B'=\pm1$, $s=A(B-B')+A'(B+B')=\pm2$ (8 of 16 sheets give $+2$); $S=E(a,b)-E(a,b')+E(a',b)+E(a',b')$; best angles $a=0,a'=90,b=45,b'=135$ give $S=-2\sqrt2\approx-2.83$. Assumptions: locality, hidden values, free choice, one outcome. Bell 1928-1990.

- Simulation lesson (21 cards): Bostrom 2003 trilemma (1) extinct before posthuman, (2) posthumans uninterested, (3) almost certainly simulated; $f_{\text{sim}}=X/(X+1)$, $X=f_Pf_IN_I$ ($H$ cancels); examples $0.01,0.01,10^6\to100/101=0.990$; $f_P=0.1,f_I=0.001$ needs $N_I=10^4$ for 50%; even credence over (1)-(3) gives 1/3; invented null test (likelihood 0.8 vs 1) takes 1/3 to 0.286. Premises: substrate independence, computing power, bland indifference $\text{Cr}=f_{\text{sim}}$. Chalmers 2022: metaphysical not skeptical. Beane-Davoudi-Savage 2012 lattice, $b^{-1}\gtrsim10^{11}$ GeV.

- Structure lesson (17 cards): pessimistic meta-induction (Laudan 1981); Worrall 1989 (Dialectica 43): Fresnel ether to Maxwell field, continuity "of form or structure, not of content"; $R=\big(\tfrac{n-1}{n+1}\big)^2$, water 1.33 gives 2.0%; epistemic (Worrall; Russell 1927 precursor) vs ontic (Ladyman 1998; Ladyman & Ross 2007) structural realism; Newman 1928 (Mind 37): $2^{n^2}$ relations on $n$ points ($n=3$: 512). Fresnel 1788-1827, Maxwell 1831-1879.
