# References, by level

Standard works to cross-check lessons against (Igor need not supply them). Prefer primary texts
or standard reference works; use the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (SEP) and the
Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy (IEP) for positions.

- **1 Big Questions:** Russell, *The Problems of Philosophy*; Nagel, *What Does It All Mean?*; SEP "Epistemology".
- **2 How Science Knows:** Hume, *Enquiry*; Popper, *Logic of Scientific Discovery*; Kuhn, *Structure of Scientific Revolutions*; Okasha, *Philosophy of Science: A Very Short Introduction*; Howson & Urbach, *Scientific Reasoning*; Ladyman, *Understanding Philosophy of Science*.
- **3 Views of Reality:** Kim, *Philosophy of Mind*; Berkeley, *Principles*; Descartes, *Meditations*; Goff, *Galileo's Error*; Ladyman & Ross, *Every Thing Must Go*; Maudlin, *Philosophy of Physics: Quantum Theory*; Bell (1964); Bostrom (2003) simulation argument.
- **4 Mathematics:** Wigner (1960); Shapiro, *Thinking about Mathematics*; Franzen, *Godel's Theorem*; Tegmark, *Our Mathematical Universe*; Cantor's diagonal argument (any analysis text).
- **5 Origins and Fine-Tuning:** Weinberg, *The First Three Minutes*; Carroll, *From Eternity to Here*; Barnes, *The Fine-Tuning of the Universe for Intelligent Life*; Rees, *Just Six Numbers*; Leslie, *Universes*; Weinberg (1987) anthropic bound; Penrose, *Road to Reality*.
- **6-7 Arguments for/against God:** Oppy, *Arguments about Gods*; Craig & Sinnott-Armstrong, *God? A Debate*; Plantinga, *God, Freedom and Evil*; Mackie, *The Miracle of Theism*; Rowe (1979); Schellenberg, *Divine Hiddenness and Human Reason*; Swinburne, *The Existence of God*; SEP entries on each argument.
- **8 Nature of God:** Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I; Maimonides, *Guide of the Perplexed*; Hartshorne; Whitehead, *Process and Reality*; Spinoza, *Ethics*; Jammer, *Einstein and Religion*; Tillich.
- **9 Science and Religion:** Barbour, *Religion and Science*; McGrath, *Science and Religion*; Gould, *Rocks of Ages*; Numbers, *Galileo Goes to Jail*; Brooke, *Science and Religion: Some Historical Perspectives*; Hume, "Of Miracles"; Polkinghorne.
- **10 Consciousness:** Chalmers, *The Conscious Mind*; Nagel (1974); Dehaene, *Consciousness and the Brain*; Tononi (IIT); Koch, *The Feeling of Life Itself*; Seth, *Being You*; Parfit, *Reasons and Persons*; Libet (1983); SEP "Consciousness".
- **11 Altered States:** Pollan, *How to Change Your Mind*; Carhart-Harris et al. (psilocybin trials); Davidson & Lutz (meditation research); Bem (2011) and replications; Alvarado; Hyman on mediumship tests; Greyson on NDEs.
- **12 Religious Experience:** James, *Varieties of Religious Experience*; Otto, *The Idea of the Holy*; Huxley, *Perennial Philosophy*; Katz; Alston, *Perceiving God*; Swinburne.
- **13 Why Humans Believe:** Boyer, *Religion Explained*; Barrett, *Why Would Anyone Believe in God?*; Norenzayan, *Big Gods*; Atran; Wilson, *Darwin's Cathedral*; Durkheim; Turner; Street (debunking).
- **14 Sacred Texts:** Ehrman; Brettler; Friedman, *Who Wrote the Bible?*; Finkelstein & Silberman, *The Bible Unearthed*; Metzger, *Text of the New Testament*; Reynolds, *The Qur'an and its Biblical Subtext*; Sinai; Neuwirth; Alter.
- **15 Hindu Thought:** Radhakrishnan; Flood, *An Introduction to Hinduism*; Olivelle, *Upanishads*; Deutsch, *Advaita Vedanta*; Bhagavad Gita (Miller, Easwaran).
- **16 Buddhist Thought:** Gethin, *Foundations of Buddhism*; Garfield, *Fundamental Wisdom of the Middle Way*; Rahula, *What the Buddha Taught*; Siderits; Wallace; Lopez.
- **17 Spiritism:** Kardec, *O Livro dos Espiritos*, *O Livro dos Mediuns*; Stevenson, *Twenty Cases Suggestive of Reincarnation*; Lewgoy; Hess, *Spirits and Scientists*; Moreira-Almeida.
- **18 Aliens:** Drake; Webb, *If the Universe Is Teeming with Aliens*; Sandberg, Drexler & Ord (2018); NASEM/AARO reports; Peters (astrotheology).
- **19 Faith and Knowledge:** Plantinga, *Warranted Christian Belief*; Clifford (1877); James (1896); Kierkegaard; Feldman & Warfield, *Disagreement*; Gettier (1963).
- **20 Meaning, Ethics, Death:** Nagel; Camus; Epicurus; Wolf, *Meaning in Life and Why It Matters*; Rachels; Murdoch.
